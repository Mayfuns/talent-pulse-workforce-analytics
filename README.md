## Project overview

Talent Pulse needed a clearer view of employee attrition across departments, age groups, tenure and other workforce segments.

Rather than relying only on raw exit counts, the analysis compares attrition proportionally across workforce groups to identify where employee loss is concentrated and where retention action could have the greatest impact.

## Business question

**Where is attrition concentrated, which employee groups are most exposed to retention risk, and where should the business investigate first?**
